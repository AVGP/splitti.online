===================================================================
                WELCOME TO SPLITTI OS 98
===================================================================

Thank you for exploring this retro web experience :)

This is the personal homepage of Martin Splitt, who you might have seen on [Bluesky](https://bsky.app/profile/martin.isdoingthings.online) or [Instagram](https://instagram.com/the_splitti) or,
if you lost control of your life, on [LinkedIn](https://www.linkedin.com/in/martinsplitt/)!

I've been on this blue planet since 1989, started out in northern Germany, moved to eastern Germany and then to Switzerland
where I [enjoy and teach scuba diving](https://divingfor.fun) and [take people's portraits](https://throughthelens.ch) (or create [photo art with old, physical and chemical processes](https://arsphotographica.ch)).


TIPS:
* Double-click (or tap on mobile) any folder icon to explore its contents.
* Click Start button to access system options.
* Drag window titlebars to move them around on desktop viewports.

Enjoy your trip back to 1998!
