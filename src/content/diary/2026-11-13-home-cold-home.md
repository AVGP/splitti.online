![Early morning arrival in Frankfurt](/assets/images/diary_frankfurt_from_the_plane.jpg)

## Winter is coming

First order of business after my wife picked me up from the airport was to get the tires changed on our car.
After a few hits&misses with other garages, I found one that is _awesome_.

They started out as the recycling company needed someone reliable to do maintenance and repairs on their fleet of trucks and special purpose vehicles.
It grew from there and they're also servicing, repairing and maintaining regular cars.

What I like about them: They're _proactive_, customer-focused and pragmatic.

With the bunch of trips [I recently did](/../2026-11-03-vienna/) in [different countries](../2026-11-10-cape-town/) for work, I kept forgetting to email or call them about getting the winter tires put on (I store them there, too).

But I didn't have to - they called me. They also let me know my winter tires are worn down, so it's time to get new ones. Minutes later I had an offer in my inbox, days later I had my appointment to get it done. _That's how it's supposed to be_. They alert me of a situation before it comes a problem, offer a solution and act on it quickly.

![In the realm of mechanics geeks](/assets/images/diary_at_the_garage.jpg)

While they went to work on the car, I had a coffee and 15 minutes later, I was out and about again.
