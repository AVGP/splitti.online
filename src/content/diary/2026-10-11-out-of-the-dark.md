Dear Diary,

The past weeks have been difficult.

I have run into depression and seasonal affective disorder all throughout my life. 

In a way, it's part of me, but most of the time, I have a good grasp on it and strong techniques and tools to keep it at bay.

Not the last couple of weeks, though. Things happened, stress mounted, sleep vanished or became disturbed, turbid rather than relaxing and opaque.

## The dark place

The voice inside me was so much louder, so much heavier than usual. It was also very convincing. It held me down, it told me that I am up against things that I _can't_ handle. Ever. 

And not only that it framed me to be in a bad, overwhelming situation and that's my own fault, it also told me something else.

It told me other's were disappointed, angry or just better off without me. It was so very convincing.

## The paradoxon of help

I felt powerless and the worst thing about it? I have fantastic friends and family to help me - but them helping me made me feel as if the voice was _absolutely right_.

It convinced me even more. It put me further down this dark place.

The more I fought it, the more I tried to do things that needed to get done or things that I normally enjoy, the more disconnected I felt from myself.

But not only that, I felt _less connected to myself_ every time I tried to reconnect with myself.

## The way out

Only thanks to medication, accepting help, this didn't wear down the last defenses.
These last defenses were shaken though. This only happens rarely, but this was one of these occasions.

I know that, once I started to feel better, I shall _under no circumstances_ get back out of recovery. Recovery for me means withdrawing from activities and from people. The last part hurts the most, but I have to.

It takes time and there's nothing to speed it up. Only once I have the perspective back and I can see the light all around me, not just ahead, I can reconnect and reconcile. 

Ironically, while withdrawing is what I need to do, I can only recover thanks to knowing that I have people around me, who'll stick to my side no matter what.

This is to say thank you to every single one of you. I know it's not easy for y'all, I know I'm asking a lot of all y'all in these times. I will never forget every single one of you, though. You're my heroes.

## Back in the light

I'm doing well right now. I'm back to seeing the light all around me, I enjoy being among the wonderful people I have in my life and I enjoy doing things I had to put aside for longer than I'm okay with.

But I know the dark place is still there. It continues calling, but in a frequency I'm no longer tuned in to. 

If you're struggling and hear the dark place calling - get all the help you can.
Even if that means putting yourself first, even if it means doing counter-intuitive things. 

You matter, don't believe the dark place.
