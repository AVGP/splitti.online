Dear diary,

Today we ran the Search Central Live in Cape Town for the first time ever.
The room was packed, vibes were great - and the venue was gorgeous!

![The colorful venue before the event started](/assets/images/diary_scl_cpt_venue.jpg)

It was [John](https://bsky.app/profile/johnmu.com) and me on stage after Marianne gave the intro and Kabelo gave the opening keynote.
So for around four hours, we delivered sessions and ended with a Q&A. We got lots of really good questions and had lovely conversations afterwards.

It always makes me happy to see people rethinking their approach when they realise that short-term tactics aren't the way to go.

## Team dinner

Team dinner was fantastic - we went to a place in the waterfront district and had great food and even better conversations.
Among our discussion of the latest AI bubble developments (who would've known that Jensen Huang meant embracing _that kind of chinese model_, huh?)
we also touched upon general business practices and the irrationality of the markets. I don't think that's a recent development ([tulips](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tulip_mania),  or [block chain](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2017-10-27/what-s-in-a-name-u-k-stock-surges-394-on-blockchain-rebrand), anyone?).

The best parts were the other conversation topics. We dived deep into cinema and series - seems like I gotta finally see [tuner](https://popfeed.social/movie/1340206) after all. Also got tips like [Widow's Bay](https://popfeed.social/tv_show/270476) and [See](https://popfeed.social/tv_show/80752)!

And we somehow hyped all of us up for the upcoming [Dune](https://popfeed.social/movie/1170608)!

There was also a hilarious conversation. You probably know the joke that a lot of people in tech jobs say "And then I quit and, idk, do something with wood".
Another option that is often mentioned is "I'll be a farmer." - turns out one of our colleagues has this as their stress-relief strategy.

They have surprisingly detailed plans of growing specific flowers for wealthy people abroad as these flowers are particularly desirable for bespoke weddings.

We also got a view of the table mountain across the harbour :)

![Table Mountain and the harbour](/assets/images/diary_table-mountain-view.jpg)

That was so much fun!
