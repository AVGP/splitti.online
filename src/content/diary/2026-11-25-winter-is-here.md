Dear diary,

today I've had the pleasure of proceeding with the next set of exercises for my Rescue Diver student.
It was great to see how she and her partner (who's doing this as a refresher) were approaching it nervously at first,
hitting their limits and then using a perspective shift to approach it much more orderly and successfully.

It was also nice to beat my inner resistance to go out into the cold and specifically to go diving again.
Diving rewires my brain in weird ways and I'm always coming out fresher, sharper, happier. Same here.

I then had pizza and a calm hangout and chat with a close friends, when an alarm for the first responder program rudely pulled me and a friend out of that.
We were first to the scene and this time it wasn't with a happy end.

But even in that horrific situation. The patient was in bed, with a book, nice and cozy and probably just left this world behind in peace.
My thoughts and my heart are with the relatives who have been thrust into this situation without warning and have to come to terms with a big change in their lives.
I wish them all the best.

So, please, cherish every moment with your loved ones. Let go of the bad things quickly, hold on to the good people and memories dearly.
