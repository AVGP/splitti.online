Dear diary,

This morning I headed to Stockholm for the Publisher Day tomorrow.
The pilot said "Weather in Stockholm is nicer than here" which turned out to be well-hidden sarcasm.

We left cold and overcast Zurich for cold and rainy Stockholm. Brrr. But it was nice nonetheless...

![My hotel room had a nook by the window!](/assets/images/diary_cozy_stockholm_hotel.jpg)

## Office day in Stockholm

As I took the Arlanda Express to Stockholm Central Station, I decided to go to the nearby Google office here.
It's a pretty office and the barista had "Apple Pie Latte". That was delicious, just like the cinnamon bun I also grabbed.

I, again, somehow skipped breakfast _and_ lunch, which isn't exactly a smart plan. 
On my hunt for a drop-in desk I ran into my fellow colleagues who are running the event I'm here for: Publisher Day Sweden.

## Cozy hotel and a great evening

After working from the office, I went to my hotel (and got lost, because there's multiple levels that you could consider "street level" and a huge shopping center).

The hotel was cute and cozy and I was delighted to find a nook in my room next to the window, so I snuggled in and read the paper they made to explain the hotel's concept and amenities before switching to reading a book. Currently reading "[Implementing Domain Driven Design](https://popfeed.social/book/9780133039887)".

Afterwards I went to dinner with some coworkers - and we managed to mostly avoid work talk - we spoke about movies. I was delighted to find that [my take on Mulholland Drive]() to be met with acceptance. I had to be forgiving about someone else's disliking of Wes Anderson and a third one confessed to not like "Interstellar" much. I think that's great - we should all be comfortable to express our preferences, thoughts and feelings. 

Well, off to bed now - tomorrow I'll be on stage, so talk to you then!
