Dear diary,

you probably know "Stayin' alive" as a song to do CPR in the right rythm, but [there are more such songs](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=0H_XUaxrna8)!

Anyway, today things took a turn when the First Responder pager app rang and summoned me to a nearby mall.
Elderly gentlemen collapsed, his wife by his side called for help. Two wonderful people from the nearby pharmacy brought an AED and started CPR until 4 minutes later
a first responder arrived, followed by my arrival and we took over. A third and fourth first responder showed up shortly after and we took turns on CPR until a few more minutes later, EMS showed up.

We got a return of circulation, the EMS assisted with oxygen, medication and breathing support and finally took the guy to the hospital. I wish him all the best!
His wife was visibly shaken, but we did the best we could offer to talk to her, calm her down, tell her options for emotional support and the care team and sat by her side during the resuscitation.
Her son picked her up afterwards. I wish them all the very best.

## We need more people to do CPR

We know that the first minutes after a cardiac arrest count. Yet, many people aren't willing to provide CPR.

That has many reasons, but I do hope, more people will do it. Take a BLS (Basic Life Support - it's CPR and a bit more) course or a refresher.
Or if you really can't be bothered to do that, at least watch this:
