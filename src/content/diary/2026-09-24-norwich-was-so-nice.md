Dear Diary,

I had the pleasure of going to Norwich for Search Norwich XL.

I arrived quite late from Warsaw where I was speaking before and I rented a car to get to Norwich.
It's wild that renting a car was not only faster but also much cheaper than taking a train - [that's not great](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Climate_crisis).

This was my first time driving a car in the UK and driving on the "wrong side" (y'all know I'm right, don't you?).
I got used to it quite quickly, but roundabouts felt odd at first.

Norwich is a quaint little city with lots of old buildings, nooks and crannies and nice places to hang out in.
I was very happy to meet many old friends (Mark, Eva, Myriam, Iva, Bengü, Yagmur, Emma, Arnout and so many more) and make new friends, like Floor!

I also did get back into doing card tricks... I definitely need more practise again but for not having done any magic in the last five years, it went reasonably well :D

The conference was lovely, including a great panel! I usually don't like panels much but this one was great.
After the conference, I made yet another set of new friends - like Lydia and Rob!

The morning afterwards felt so relieving and refreshing. I've had a fantastic time, but I'm also very happy to be back home.
