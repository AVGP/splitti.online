Dear Diary,

Everyone at school and on Usenet keeps talking about the "Y2K Bug". Mr. Henderson in computer science class says older banking mainframes only store two digits for the year, so when 1999 rolls over to 00, computers might think it's 1900!

Mom bought a few extra cans of soup just in case. Luckily, Splitti OS 98 has 4-digit date support built in! I checked system control panel date settings and it seems safe.

Gotta go, Dad needs to make a phone call so I have to disconnect the internet.
