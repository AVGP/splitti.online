Dear diary,

today a friend and I visited [Volumes Zurich](https://www.volumeszurich.ch/). I didn't exactly know what to expect, but it promised zines, so I was down.
It was packed and a bit smaller than I thought, but it was interesting. I bought a bunch of zines and I got inspired.

My highlight was [an old copy press](https://www.instagram.com/p/Dfu2FfPukIl/) that [Balthazar Wyss](https://www.instagram.com/balthazarwyss/) let us use.
He also made a wild stereoscopic zine.

## Walk and talk

Afterwards we had the first Fondue of the year and then walked around, taking photos around the city. It was a lovely conversation - from films and books to photography and philosophy,
AI and work - we covered a lot of ground.

As usual when the two of us meet, haha. I'm really looking forward to hanging out again next week.

We'll see.

Otherwise, I'll take this as a slow, recharging weekend. Cheers!
