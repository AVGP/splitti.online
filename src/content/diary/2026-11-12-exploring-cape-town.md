Dear diary,

Cape Town is [a hoot](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=nAS-_W3JDd0)!

## Waterfront

![Sunset at Waterfront](/assets/images/diary_waterfront_sunset.jpg)

It's a vibrant area, quite touristy to be honest - but still nice. There is an active harbour, lots of active ship yards too.
I was quite surprised to find a bridge I went across three times before suddenly closing and _swinging around_ to let a boat pass through the channel.

I really liked the old silo architecture that has been preserved and upgraded for hotels and other venues!

![The Silo hotel. Fancy pants!](/assets/images/diary_the_silo.jpg)

## WiFi like it's 1995!

I wasn't staying in the fancy pants "The Silo", but close by in the Radisson Red. Lovely hotel, lovely staff - and a new WiFi system.
Hilariously, my room had probably one of the worst WiFi speeds I've ever encountered. I checked if they have a paid, faster option.
I already was on the supposedly faster WiFi, but uhhh...

![Holy 56k modem, Batman!](/assets/images/diary_hotel_wifi.jpg)

## Hop on, hop off

You've probably seen these red busses taking loads of tourists through cities around the world.
They are a bit hit-and-miss, some places have terribly shitty routes, some others are great.

![A view of the ocean and the city from our way up Table Mountain](/assets/images/diary_table_mountain_view.jpg)

I've taken them in places like Barcelona, Lisbon and a few other places - so I naturally had a look at the routes in Cape Town, too.
I generally prefer exploring on my own, but with the mixed bag of experiences in Johannesburg and the little time I had between finishing work and my flight leaving, I thought I'd give it a go.

![I don't know why, but the mountain/ocean combination just does it for me](/assets/images/diary_capetown_oceanview.jpg)

I took the red route and it has been _great_! I saw a bunch of the city, went up Table Mountain and down to the beaches, so that was great!

## I wanna come back!

It's been so nice. People have been friendly, weather has been gentle (in contrast to foggy, cold, wet Zurich at the same time).
I wanna come back and spend more time here - I hear diving is great around this region as well, and the national parks must be lovely, too!
