Dear diary,

today I woke up in a different country than I fell asleep in and got into "the flow" like I haven't been able to in a long time.

## Choo choo!

I took a night train and for the first time I tried a ["mini cabin"](https://www.youtube.com/shorts/oL1kEhNEjX0) and had a pretty solid night's sleep.

In Vienna, my hotel room would only be available from 3pm, so I spent the day at Zoku and had _all the tea_ and a great time sitting in a comfy nook and working.

![My small kingdom en route to Vienna](/assets/images/diary_nightjet_mini_cabin.jpg)

## Zoku - the living room far away from home

I got a lot of writing, coordinating and planning done. Once I was done working, I also took the opportunity to look a little closer into different LLMs and local LLM options.
But not only that - I also thought a bit about what my focus areas should be for 2027, what goals I want to spend more time on in 2027 and I made a few strategic decisions for the future, too.

## In the tunnel

Things have been strange for a while. I mean, [due to one thing](/content/diary/2026-10-11-out-of-the-dark) or [another](/content/2026-10-17-mixed-signals) and more throughout the last year (or maybe even years? I lost track, to be honest), I rarely had the flow, the zone, the focus that I enjoy so much.

Or rather, if I had it, it often was carrying me "astray" from where I should've been. Well, where others would say I should've been.
Again I am reminded today how I'm weird (in a good way!) and need to [take control](/content/diary/2026-10-22-seeing-the-light) of my path.

This feels more and more liberating every day.
