Dear Diary,

Today I finally had some time to make art.
I set out to make some wetplate landscape shots, tintypes basically.
But after scouting for a bit, I didn't find a spot that felt right.

I ended up going to the darkroom instead and made prints. It was so nice!
It's refreshing and beautiful and brings peace to my mind.

I really enjoy having the power to capture a moment and then create fine art prints from these moments.
It allows me to focus on a moment and work with physical media, which is a slower process than other things I deal with.
