Dear Diary,

I had a hard time falling asleep yesterday. But not because of something bad or the thoughts spinning in my head.

It was the same feeling I had as a child before Christmas. I just couldn't wait for the night to be over and the day to finally arrive!
I had that until recent years, whenever something truly excited me. It has been gone for a while and I'm taken aback that I didn't even notice that before last night.

I _felt_ something. I felt liberated in the last couple of days; but I also felt weary of the future, worried about my decisions. Those I made, those I'll have to make.
But this was the feeling of pure, unbridled excitement. I hold ideas in my head and have the strength, liberty, skills and knowledge to build them, to make them real.

Wildly enough, among the couple of reasonable, interesting ideas that will provide actual value, silly and stupid ideas crept in as well.
These are fun and you know what? I might make them happen, too. Why not?

But now, woken up, dressed and ready to take on the world, I will try to balance the day with something crafty, something artsy, some reading (Started reading "The Process", Kafka. Great story!) and some building as well.

Toodles!
