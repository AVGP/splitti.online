Dear diary,

today was a day with new people and new experiences.

I went to a foto challenge in Zurich. There were 5 other photographers and 6 models. Each model got a challenge (some with props) and there were three rounds for a total of 6 challenges.
We split up in teams of two photographers and two models - taking turns so every photographer did a shooting with every model.

I really liked that part: The models I met were all wonderful people and we had a great time together, exploring what photos we could create together in the timeframe and with the given challenge.

## Aut veniam aut faciam

Yet, I don't know what it was but I picked up some undercurrent that I didn't vibe with from the organiser. That might just be me being weird, though. It happens.
I'll be back for another photo event with the same organiser in December, we'll see how it goes.

But I'm observing carefully, I'm learning what I enjoy and what I'd do differently. I know I can build communities and stages for others to shine on. Why not do that for photography, too?
Just wondering which vessel. I'm part of [Photographers International Club Zurich]() (aka "PICZ") after all, so maybe I'll do something under that umbrella. 
Then there's hob-hob, but that has a slightly different vibe and focus. Or maybe I kick off my own endeavour. We will see.

Anyway, my love for photography remains strong. Seeing how differently my own perspective, my own eyes guide my decisions on how to portray a person is just such an exciting journey every time.

## OeBB Nightjet and mini cabins

Another new experience was that I decided to take the night train to Vienna. I've done that before and it's generally a pleasant experience. Get on board, make yourself comfortable, sleep and wake up in a different country.

But Nightjets aren't cheap. At least not, if the "sleep" part of the journey matters to you. I got lucky once and got a 2-bed cabin for myself, but again: That was luck.
Now I don't mind another person in a bed above or beyond mine or across a small aisle. BUT: I snore and when I do it's in world-ending volume. That's just a dick move to the other passenger.

So I was quite excited to learn about the "mini cabin" concept they've recently launched. It's like a capsule hotel. Interestingly people fawn about the idea of the Japanese compact hotels, 
but the response I got to sharing my "mini cabin" (they really should call it a capsule, tbh) with friends was "OMG".
As if I just sat down next to a drug addict in the redlight district and mentioned that I carry large swads of cash on my person and will now fall asleep so deeply that I won't notice anything.

But I was fairly happy except for the joke of a "pillow". That was as if an alien who's never seen one up close was described the rough idea of a pillow by a very easily-distractable five year old child.

Anyway, I settled in, put my phone onto the wireless charging pad, took out my e-ink reader to sink back into Kafka's ["The Trial"](https://thatsaweb.site/films/the-trial/).
Soon after I would go brush my teeth and get ready to "sink into" the joke of a pillow, drifting into the realm of Morpheus. Luckily I got the greek god, not the Matrix dude.
