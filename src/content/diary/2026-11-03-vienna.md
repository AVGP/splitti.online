Dear diary,

It's only now that I truly realise that I've been a fish out of the water.

I was in Vienna today for a conference, now I'm on the night train back home - and the day was lovely.
The conference was fine, nothing special to be frank - except for the opening which was a lovely violinist giving us a great solo performance. You don't see that every day!
But overall, I'm not a big fan of conferences that open with a promo video of their own conference - I mean, huh? 
It's the kind of showmanship that I find offputting, but that's probably just me.

While I was here, I got a wonderful email - one I've been waiting for since last week. It contains paperwork... if you know me, you know I hate paperwork.
But this paperwork is going to bring me back into my natural habitat: Solving problems, building solutions, helping people through mentoring, coaching and education.

It's liberating to see the fog lifting off your future. The path slowly becoming clearer.

![Even in the densest forest, sunlight illuminates the path ahead](/assets/images/photo_foggy-forest-path.jpg)

The clarity is exhilarating. I know there'll be obstacles and challenges, but these are _mine_. 
I own them, I get to choose my approach, I regained _autonomy_.

I'm really excited for the things ahead!
