Dear diary,

after a long duo of flights from Zurich to Frankfurt and then to Cape Town, I've arrived in my hotel :)
Oh, also saw a rat at the food court in Frankfurt airport - not exactly helpful when you're considering to grab a bite there.

Anyway, food on the flight was great and the flight itself was pretty smooth.
I sunk into the last chapters of "The Trial". I almost forgot how much I don't like to read Kafka, but hey. I finished the book after all.

I've decided to [put out my CV](https://splitti.online/cv/) in a, uhm, unusual form. Always good to meet opportunities as they come.

## 2027

I took some of the flight time (as the WiFi wasn't working) to think about what I want out of 2027.
I definitely want to do more swimming. Time to get back on that. Diving will continue, of course. Oh and the same for photography.

But I also want to continue what I did this year: Spend time with family and friends. 
Reading at least a book a month is a good goal...many months this year I finished two or three books. 
It helps to set that goal - when I catch myself mindlessly scrolling, I read instead. It's _so much better_.

I wanna lose weight and keep an eye on my blood pressure, too. In corpore sana, mens sana.

## Ready

I realised how much calm I carry in me these days. From being inspired from books and conversations with friends and acquaintances
to enjoying time with the people closest to me and making art together. It feels good to be back and back at peace.

I'm ready for 2027 and I learned my lesson regarding pacing - while many things could happen faster, I'm pacing myself. I do the leg work now and take the big waves starting January.
