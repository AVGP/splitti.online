Dear Diary,

I recently thought a lot about a lot of different things.
One of these things is where I should be and if that's the place where I am.
I don't mean geographically - [Zurich is wonderful](/content/photos/photo7-zurich-night/) - I'm talking about where to spend my time and talents.

After having experienced places that changed and where I didn't belong any more, I came up with a question I ask myself periodically.

I call it the **lottery test**: What would I do, if I won the lottery and didn't need to work for a while anymore?

Usually the answer to the question is - and at my current job has for almost 9 years been - I'd continue just the same. It mainly boils down to the people I work with and the work I get to do.

But this started slipping. First I thought of taking a break, then reducing to 80% or even 50%. But recently, that has changed fundamentally.

Why? What happened? 
Mixed signals.

On one hand, I'm getting told that there are issues, lapses - missed tickets, miscommunications. Not enough work being done. Projects not complex enough.
On the other hand, I'm being thanked for the work I do, praised for how I'm doing it and how much impact it has.

Then we're discussing what to do differently - and all I hear is: You're not enough, you have to do more. And you're not doing the right things, you have to do things differently and focus on different things.

## I'm not _right_

I love my work, I love solving problems, being an engineer and educator is perfect. So, when I'm getting feedback that I'm not doing things right, that I should do things differently, that's first and foremost a challenge.

But my personality traits, like everything in life, has two sides. I enjoy engaging with problems and solve them through engineering or through education.
I bring self-motivation, creativity and high-velocity problem solving to the table. But there's a flip side: I suffer (and I truly mean _suffer_) from overhead and reporting. I know it sounds silly - nobody likes reporting, right? But for me that's different. 

Go on three different stages in a single week with travel in between, presenting a new slide deck each time in front of hundreds of people? Cool. Build a new tool in a few weeks in a system and programming language I'm not familiar with? Let's gooo! 

But write up a list of things I've done in the past quarter and match it to my job description? That's like asking me to climb Mt. Everest without oxygen.
It makes my _physically sick_. I don't know why, I don't know what to do about it. It's been like that my whole life.

But everyone else can do it? Why can't I? What is wrong with me? Why can't I be good enough for this job? I wanna change but it just makes me more miserable.

## Me is all I got

That's when it hit me. I'm in the wrong place. It wasn't like that in the past, but the place has changed, I guess? I struggle, because I'm not where I belong. A fish out of the water, suddenly. The tide has turned, I guess?

But I found my footing again. I'm not wrong, I'm not "not enough". I'm exactly who I have always been, will always be.
I'm grateful for the opportunities I had in the last decade and beyond - I grew, I improved, I learned new things. 
Of course, that included me failing, making mistakes, having to course correct and go through rough periods - that's part of life and part of growth.

But some things haven't changed; I am still me. Creative, sometimes stubborn. Pragmatic, sometimes lazy. Innovative, sometimes chaotic. 

That's me. I bring a bunch of good things to any table that allows me to sit at it.

But this isn't my table anymore.

## What's ahead?

I don't know. I'm thinking about this, listening to my head and my heart. I wanna explore a bunch of things and I want to define a few traits of the place I'm seeking, so I can make a good decision next.

I know I want to do something that matters with the skills I got. I also want to deliver results with the customer in mind. Not stakeholders, not investors - I want to be held accountable by those I'm building for. I want to do something that aligns with my values. And I wanna do it with people I trust, can learn from and be my whole self with.

I won't wear a mask again. I'm not too much, I am who I am.
