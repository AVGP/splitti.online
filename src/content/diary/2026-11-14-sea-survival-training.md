## Sea Survival Training

Why did I drive to a pool in the morning? For [Sea Surival Training](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=LGt3pwOgRBY) of all things.
We learned about the priorities of survival, how long it _usually_ takes until you can expect rescue and why keeping the boat afloat as long as possible is a good idea and worthy investment of energy.

Once we climbed into a life raft we understood that on a _physical level_.

Not gonna lie: I didn't think too much of this training. How card could it be?

Turns out: Quite hard. And this was in easiest, optimal conditions: It was bright, it was warm water and air and basically no waves or currents. HOLY FUCK.
