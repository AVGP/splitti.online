Dear diary,

I ain't gonna sugarcoat it: I didn't feel good this morning. The weather is a drab, I had a difficult conversation this morning (but a clarifying one).
But then it all turned.

# Old and new friends and great conversations

First I got a voice message that made me happy and curious, then I got to take a few steps in the right direction.
Then the best thing: I got to talk to a few people I really enjoyed meeting and talking with - it was a great time, in fact.
I also reconnected with someone I hadn't talked to in quite some time - that was refreshing and felt really good!

Then I got a result I didn't expect (a lot faster than anticipated) and I realised that things were indeed looking up.
Another opportunity appeared on the horizont, too.

## Emails emails emails

I did have to send a few emails that I'm not too happy about, but they're inevitable. Namely: We have three slots for community speakers at Search Central Live Zurich.
There were 28 submissions and with very few exceptions they were all great. The ones that weren't great were still pretty good - so picking was very difficult.
I even made sure we had space to accomodate a fourth talk in our agenda, but it still meant 24 people would receive sad news.

I'll try to make the most out of the situation for those people by offering to look over their slides and their pitch and provide feedback, so it wasn't for nothing.
Still, doesn't feel great. But that's the reality of running events. I'm actually tempted to do something like "reject.js" did and invite some of them to do their talks in a different forum, 
but it's not enough time to do that right now. We'll see how I'll deal with that.

## Looking forward

I'm looking forward to things to come, I'm excited for what the rest of the week will bring and I'm grateful for the people around me.
