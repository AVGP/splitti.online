## The Lighthouse & anxious people

I also watched The Lighthouse. That was on my list for a while and I finally got around to watch it with Anne.
I have so many thoughts and feelings about that movie, omg. I had to [let them out in writing, over at the other online sanctuary that I pour my heart into](https://thatsaweb.site/films/the-lighthouse/) and this would follow me for the rest of the week. IDK why I'd ever watch some soulless AI shit, when art like this exists. Oh, and it was so much better than Mulholland Drive, about which [I also have thoughts and feelings](https://thatsaweb.site/films/mulholland-drive/), albeit of a different nature.

I also stumbled upon a book from Fredrik Backman, [Anxious People](https://popfeed.social/book/1405930268), which apparently now also is a Netflix series. Whatever.

I _expected_ the book to be a fun, light read and enjoyed the warm embrace it started off with...until it _stabbed me in the back_ from within that very embrace. It took me on an unexpected emotional rollercoaster right until the last page. I didn't know I needed that, but I did. I laughed, I cried and I enjoyed every moment of that. I don't remember the last time a book made me cry. Except for books on theoretical computer science and financial maths - they don't count. 

I'll have to write a review for that book, but I'll be frank: Still harboring strong emotions after that read, so I'll need to have a bit of a lie-down first.

## Choosing violence

I have long realised that a good week contains making some art or doing some crafts.
This week I printed a bunch of zines, folded little envelopes and closed them up with self-printed stickers.

This was the first time I worked with a Cricut plotter and cutter. That was a bit of a learning curve, but I think that's because I got nervous and overwhelmed by all the information that was available to me. It turned out to be quite simple to get going and [make my own stickers](https://youtube.com/shorts/nVi91RVcIsM?feature=share).

I got the package with 24 zines in 24 envelopes, sealed with 24 self-made stickers to the post office for the amazing zine advent calendar from [Jenni](https://zines.cool/). Of course last minute, _damn my eyes_.

![The homemade envelopes for the zine advent calendar](/assets/images/retros/26-44-zine-calendar.jpg)

I also sent off the zines to the lovely people from the [r/zines subreddit](https://reddit.com/r/zines) who wanted to trade zines with me!
Excited for the zines they'll send over :)

I also finally had the courage to fold some of the riso-printed zines I did. I've used a risograph in the past, including two-color prints. But this was my first 4-color (CMYK) print and the first time I'd make a zine from those prints. 

![The homemade envelopes for the zine advent calendar](/assets/images/retros/26-44-riso-zine-fails.jpg)

The first thing I learned: Be _very_ careful to get the color layers in the right order. The colors in the photo are all wrong. Good job!
Well, whenever we make a mistake, we get closer to the right way of doing things, I guess.

Oh, I also learned that _no matter how long_ you dry these prints, the color _will_ rub off at the first chance. Sigh. Well, each one a unique piece.
