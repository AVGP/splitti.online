What a week it's been since the last retro.

## Vienna

As the week started, I woke up in Vienna. Took the night train and then worked throughout the day from there.

That same evening I took the night train back after a lovely day of presentations, Q&A and hallway chats.

I also published on of the most difficult blog posts I've written.
But I care about [men's health and I'm joining Movember this year](https://thatsaweb.site/posts/month-of-the-mo/), so there we go.

## The family you choose

I also had a great time connecting with the people closest to me. Still coughing, I stayed home to work and enjoyed the company of my lovely wife.
In Vienna I had a lovely call with my brother from another mother, my sister popped over for laughs and chats and then we all had a great dinner at our favourite restaurant. That's a great week right there.

As if that wasn't enough, I got to help to very close friends with their projects and I hope I made enough of an impact for them there.
I also had a good heart-to-heart with a dear friend who's helped me tremendously in the last couple of weeks without even realising it, I think.

So, let's make sure to let the people know when they impact you in meaningful ways.

## Return of the flow

This week the flow came back, I got into the zone and made massive headway on a couple of ideas. These might turn out important building blocks for what's to come. Finally got [Search Central Live Zurich](https://rsvp.withgoogle.com/events/scl-zurich-26?pli=1) published and announced. Great to see there are so many resources available for getting this blog post out. "Gut Ding will Weile haben" as we say in German.

## Blankets and tea

![Restaurant seating amid construction work](/assets/images/retros/26-45-cozy.jpg)

I started the week with lots of tea and a cozy nook to work in and I finished it with tea on the couch.

In between I had the lovely chance to reconnect with someone I knew since I was 6 years old - we had a walk, a tea and a cinnamon bun and the twenty years (holy moly!) we hadn't seen each other were just blown away. I really enjoyed this, because I feel we're both very similar people. Soft hearts, strong emotions and a brain that sees things differently than many others do. He made very different decisions along the way, but I always liked him a lot. I'm so happy to see he's finally realised his potential and made something of it.

After all the travel and building for the present and future, it was time to plop down on the couch and recharge. [Ted Lasso](https://popfeed.social/tv_show/97546) was just what I was looking for. Feel-good cozy, watchable stuff. Nice and easy. Plus the moustache Jason Sudeikis sports in his lead role is aspirational for my Movember journey.

So, peace out, have a great day and let's roll into the next week like a storm rolls in from the ocean.
