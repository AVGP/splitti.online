## ATmospheric turbulences

I've moved my ATproto data server to Eurosky a while ago... and they had [an oopsie](https://eurosky.tech/transparency/incidents/2026-11-storage-disruption/) this week.
No biggie, but I wasn't too worried anyway - I can always move my data back out.

Which I then did - not because Eurosky isn't great, but I discovered [teal.fm](https://teal.fm/profile/splitti.online) and wanted to move all the ~200k records into my PDS.
As every reasonable PDS, they had a rate limit, so it would've taken _days_ to move my data there. Instead I set up a PDS, moved my data over there, imported all my listening history, 
enabled rate limiting again and now can't be arsed to move back to Eurosky. We'll see.

## Spreading my wings

I've had many interesting and insightful conversations. I met old friends, made new friends, learned a bunch of new things and got to go into problem-solving mode. That was very refreshing.
I also went to [Volumes Zurich]() where I talked to a few artists and bought a few zines. That was nice and refreshing.

Also had the first fondue of the year, hah!

A bunch of things are in motion now and progressing towards the next chapter. I am very excited but I'd be lying if I pretended not to be a bit scared as well. 

_What if things go wrong?_

But then I thought about something far worse: What if _things stayed like they were before?_ No, that's no good at all, time for change, time to embrace the new and deal with the unknown.

With that, off to a fresh week!
