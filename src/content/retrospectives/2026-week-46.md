## Animal kingdom

It started off with a short hop to Frankfurt, where I saw a rat at the food court of the airport.
Not exactly a great start. But hey, the lounge was comfy and I got some work done before the longer flight.

Funnily enough, I might just be a Disney princess - cause during my lunch in Cape Town two days later, this bird came closer than I'd be happy about.

![This bird was HUNGRY](/assets/images/diary_feathered_food_thief.jpg)

## Cape Town

In Cape Town we got to hang out with the lovely team of Google South Africa and Search Central Live brought together many people from the region to talk about Search, AI and the web ecosystem. I enjoyed the nice conversations in the breaks and after the event the most.

![Looking down Table Mountain towards the Ocean](/assets/images/diary_table_mountain_view.jpg)

Cape Town itself gave me a few hours for sightseeing before heading out to the airport again and it treated me well - Table Mountain has gorgeous views, the ocean front has lots of great beaches. A sight to behold!

The flight back had no working internet, again... the way to Cape Town was the same story. So time to read. 

## Kafka and me

After finishing "The Trial" from Kafka, I dug into [Domain Driven Design Distilled](https://popfeed.social/book/8550821241).
And what can I say? The Trial had me laughing, shaking my head and comparing it to too many situations I've run into. But it's still Kafka and I'm not exactly fond of his style of storytelling. The story itself is great, tho, recommend a read. It's just not gonna be a comfy one.

I also watched [Roofman](https://thatsaweb.site/films/roofman/) and "Silence of the lambs" on my flight back.

## Home, sweet home!

![Photo prints and friendly people](/assets/images/diary_prints_and_drinks.jpg)

Back home I got more bits and pieces in a row for the upcoming adventures that I hopefully soon be talking more about, we'll see.
One of these that I'm really excited about is a document a friend sent me. It contains ideas and research and most importantly: _questions_.
Questions I need to ask, questions I don't have readily available answers. These questions are what I'm really, really excited about.

I also got to talk about photos, prints and zines with a bunch of lovely and interesting people. That was a fun evening!

Then, Saturday, I went to a Sea Survival Training with a friend and that punched me in the guts more than I'd expected.
Life rafts and I now have a more complicated relationship than before.

Sunday was relaxing... and typing this up! Oh, and yes, [The Mo](https://thatsaweb.site/posts/month-of-the-mo/) is still a go!
