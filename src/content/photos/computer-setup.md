### Ultimate Battlestation 1998

Finally upgraded the machine! 

**Specs:**
- Intel Pentium II Processor @ 350MHz
- 64 MB SDRAM
- 8.4 GB Ultra ATA Hard Drive
- 3DFX Voodoo2 12MB Graphics Accelerator
- 32x CD-ROM Drive & 56k V.90 Modem

The 17-inch CRT monitor weighs about 40 pounds but the color rendering in *Unreal* and *Quake II* is mind blowing!
