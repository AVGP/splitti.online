### Kodak Moment - Summer '98

Spent the whole afternoon watching the tide roll in. Dropped off the film at the 1-Hour Photo lab down at the pharmacy and picked up these prints. The orange and pink gradients in the sky came out really vibrant! 

*Note: Kept this print in my photo album under my desk.*
